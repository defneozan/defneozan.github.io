I am a postdoctoral researcher in the <a href="https://www.imperial.ac.uk/aeronautics/">Department of Aeronautics</a> at Imperial College London, where I am working with <a href="https://profiles.imperial.ac.uk/a.wynn/about">Dr. Andrew Wynn</a> on the state estimation of chaotic fluid flows from partial observations. I completed my PhD in the same department with <a href="https://www.lucamagri.com/">Prof. Luca Magri</a>, on scientific machine learning for modelling and optimisation of fluids, with a special focus on thermoacoustics. During this time, I was also part of the <a href="https://aifornetzero.co.uk/">AI for Net Zero in Energy and Transport</a> project as a research assistant.

My research interests lie in optimal control and observer design for nonlinear partial differential equations, where I explore machine learning to bridge data-driven and model-based approaches for complex and high-dimensional systems.
